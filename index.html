About Annika

I grew up in a kitchen with the lights on low.

Born in Denmark, raised on rye bread and quiet winter afternoons. I carried that with me into real estate: the belief that a home is first a feeling, and only after that a square-footage figure.

My clients tend to be people who want the process to feel human. We meet for coffee. We walk houses together. We talk about light, neighbours, where the wind comes from in February. Then — when we're sure — we do the spreadsheet bit, and we do it well.

— Annika
